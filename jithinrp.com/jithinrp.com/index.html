About KUG Coimbatore

KUG Coimbatore is an official JetBrains-recognised Kotlin User Group based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We bring together developers, students, and tech enthusiasts who are passionate about the Kotlin programming language and its ecosystem — from Android and backend to Kotlin Multiplatform and beyond.

Whether you are just starting out or shipping production Kotlin code, this is your local home base. We host meetups, workshops, live coding sessions, and online talks — all free and open to everyone.
